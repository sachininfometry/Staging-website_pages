Client

Connecting the solar customer and asset lifecycle

Sunrun is a leading residential solar and energy services company with business processes spanning customer acquisition, sales, partner operations, contracts, solar production, customer engagement, project finance, credit, and portfolio management.

As the business scaled, these processes generated significant data across Salesforce, Oracle applications, meter and production systems, contract databases, and homegrown applications. Sunrun engaged Infometry to unify this information and provide trusted insights across the business.

The challenge

From fragmented systems to a trusted data foundation

Analytics spanned the entire customer and asset lifecycle, but the data was distributed across multiple operational platforms.

Fragmented enterprise data

CRM, ERP, production, finance, meter-management, contract, and operational data lived across disconnected systems.

Multiple sources of truth

Customer, home, contract, partner, and production records lacked a single, trusted analytical view.

Complex data structures

Historical proposals and operational information required extensive alignment and transformation.

Data quality & consistency

Customer, contact, partner, equipment, and financial data needed repeatable quality controls.

Cross-functional analytics

Sales, operations, production, customer engagement, and finance needed connected insights.

Growing analytical complexity

NPV, credit, project economics, and portfolio performance demanded a scalable foundation.

Sunrun needed more than isolated reporting. It needed an enterprise data foundation that could connect processes and create consistent business metrics.

The Infometry solution

A unified, analytics-ready enterprise architecture

Infometry implemented an end-to-end data warehouse, orchestration, and analytics platform designed around Sunrun's business processes—not isolated source systems.
